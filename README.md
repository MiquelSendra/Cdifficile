# Nosocomial *Clostridioides difficile* Infections: Risk Factors and Outcomes

**Epidemiology, risk factors, and outcomes of nosocomial *Clostridioides difficile* infections: a cohort study in a tertiary hospital**

## Overview

This repository contains the analysis and data processing code for a retrospective cohort study on *Clostridioides difficile* infection (CDI) in hospitalized patients during 2023.
The study includes 251 CDI cases and aims to identify key risk factors associated with reinfection and mortality, with special focus on treatment regimens, clinical history, and the presence of hypervirulent ribotypes such as RT-181.

## Objectives

- Identify clinical, analytical, and therapeutic variables linked to CDI reinfection and mortality.
- Develop a predictive tool to support clinical decision-making.

## Key Findings

- Vancomycin treatment is associated with increased mortality.
- Impaired renal function correlates with higher risk of death and reinfection.
- Ribotype RT-181 is significantly associated with reinfection.
- A nomogram model estimates patient risk based on input variables.
